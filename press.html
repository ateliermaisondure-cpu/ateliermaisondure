Institutional Responses & References / Réponses et références institutionnelles

22 September 2026 – Ministry of Culture of Ukraine responds to Maison du Regard Open Expert Report

22 septembre 2026 – Le ministère de la Culture de l’Ukraine répond à l’Open Expert Report de Maison du Regard

September / Septembre 2026 | Ukraine

Read the institutional reference / Lire la référence institutionnelle
On 22 September 2026, Tetiana Krupa, cultural heritage expert at Transcultural Atelier – Maison du Regard, received an official response from the Ministry of Culture of Ukraine concerning her independent Open Expert Report, Cultural Heritage and Tourism in Ukraine’s Post-War Recovery: The Risk of Institutional Capture and Opportunities for Local Development .
The official letter, signed by Deputy Minister Yevhen Lavro, confirms that the Ministry examined the proposals presented in the report and will take them into account in its further work in the relevant areas. Maison du Regard values this professional feedback and the continuation of institutional dialogue concerning cultural heritage, post-war recovery, transparency and the role of local communities.
***
Le 22 septembre 2026, Tetiana Krupa, experte en patrimoine culturel auprès du Transcultural Atelier – Maison du Regard , a reçu une réponse officielle du ministère de la Culture de l’Ukraine concernant son Open Expert Report indépendant, Cultural Heritage and Tourism in Ukraine’s Post-War Recovery: The Risk of Institutional Capture and Opportunities for Local Development .
La lettre officielle, signée par le vice-ministre Yevhen Lavro, confirme que le ministère a examiné les propositions formulées dans le rapport et les prendra en compte dans la poursuite de ses travaux dans les domaines concernés. Maison du Regard accorde une grande importance à ce retour professionnel ainsi qu’à la poursuite du dialogue institutionnel autour du patrimoine culturel, de la reconstruction d’après-guerre, de la transparence et du rôle des communautés locales.

Institutional reference / Référence institutionnelle

  • Ministry of Culture of Ukraine. Official response concerning the independent Open Expert Report by Tetiana Krupa, 22 September 2026. Signed by Deputy Minister Yevhen Lavro.

July / Juillet 2026 | EU

Read the full news / Lire la nouvelle complète
Maison du Regard sincerely thanks Day.Az and journalist Leyla Tariverdiyeva for their thoughtful attention to our research platform and to the work of the international expert community engaged in documenting and analysing cultural heritage in contexts of conflict and post-conflict recovery.
We remain committed to international academic cooperation, methodological dialogue, and the responsible study and protection of cultural heritage. We hope that continued collaboration between researchers, institutions, journalists, and heritage professionals will contribute to preserving our shared cultural heritage for future generations.
